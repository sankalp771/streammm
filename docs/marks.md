Judging Process & Rubrics Criteria
Max total: 400 points. No peer voting this edition. Everything is scored against the rubric below, plus bonus points awarded by judges during demos.
 How it works
Basic Points (100) - required to be considered for prizes. You can still pitch without them, but you can't win. Say all four out loud during your pitch: repo link, contract address, live URL, deployment.
Advance Points (200) - this is where you actually win. Half for a product that works, half for getting people to see it.
Bonus Points (100) - extra credit. Some verified by organizers, some awarded live by judges.
Everything locks at 5:45 PM (submission close). Links, screenshots, view counts - whatever is submitted by then is what counts. Posts after the deadline earn nothing.
